How to Play HEX

Objective

HEX is a two-player connection game. Each player tries to create an unbroken chain of their stones connecting their two opposite sides of the board.

Players

  • Player 1 (Red) connects Top to Bottom
  • Player 2 (Blue) connects Left to Right

Rules

  • Players take turns placing one stone on any empty cell
  • Once placed, stones cannot be moved or removed
  • The first player to connect their sides wins
  • There are no draws in HEX - someone must win!

Strategy Tips

  • Control the center of the board for more connection options
  • Block your opponent while building your own path
  • Look for "bridge" patterns - two stones that can always connect
